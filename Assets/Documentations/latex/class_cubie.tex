\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a single cubie (small cube) of the Rubik\textquotesingle{}s Cube. 

Each cubie stores the color index of its 6 faces. The cubie itself does not know its spatial position; that is handled by \doxylink{class_cube_state}{Cube\+State}.

Rotation affects only the ordering of its face colors.
